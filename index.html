ABOUT US

About College

Ucch Madhyamik Vidyalay Phulsangavi is a Higher Secondary educational institution focused on providing quality Science education to students of Classes XI and XII.

Our academic approach aims to build strong conceptual understanding, scientific thinking, discipline and confidence. Students are encouraged to learn through classroom teaching, practical work, regular assessment and guidance for higher education.

The institution is committed to supporting students as they prepare for Higher Secondary Board examinations and future academic opportunities in Science and related fields.

Our Focus

  • Strong foundation in Science subjects
  • Practical and activity-based learning
  • Board examination preparation
  • Student discipline and development
  • Guidance for higher education
